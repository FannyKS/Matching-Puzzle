How to play

Field manual

  • Flip. Every card starts face down. Click one to turn it over and enlarge it in the inspector, along with its true position (row/column) and its four neighbours.
  • Score. Each card you turn for the first time costs points. Good runs are the ones where a sharp eye beats a thorough sweep.
  • Host. Players say the name out loud. The host presses Game complete. The reveal card then moves on to the next photo in the library, or offers the same photo again. The mic button is optional — it just writes down what was said.
  • Modes. Advanced scrambles the 30 cards — flip cards and use the inspector to piece the photo together. Easy keeps the photo assembled on the board and lets players reveal up to 12 pieces in place.
  • Library. Photos come from a folder you choose. Add, rename or move files any time and press Rescan folder; names you have edited are remembered.
  • Intel. The category hint is free. Turning a card over for them, or narrowing to three options, costs points but can save the run.
  • Keyboard. ←→↑↓ to move between cards, Enter to complete the round, Esc to close.
